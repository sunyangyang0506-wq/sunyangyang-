# Where to find English teaching jobs

Treat these pages as starting points. On every run, find the live role and its application instructions. Deduplicate the same job across sites. Expand beyond these examples with the searches below.

| Track | Start here | What to verify |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Online from abroad | [eHello recruiting](https://www.ehailuo.com/teacher/recruit/index.html) and [teaching roles](https://www.ehailuo.com/teacher/recruit/accreditedteacher.html) | Check for adult/business assignments, allowed countries, Beijing teaching hours, whether a rate is per lesson, and a functioning application path. Pages can mix adult and children's courses. |
| Online from abroad | [VIPKid eligibility](https://www.vipkid.com/teach/teacher-requirements) and [application process](https://www.vipkid.com/teach/application-process) | Check current work-authorization and residence requirements, the specific program, student location and age. A global teacher intake does not guarantee mainland students, assignments or booked hours. |
| In person, language schools | [English 1 China placements](https://teaching.english1.cn/work-abroad/teaching-english-in-china/) and [application](https://www.english1.com/apply-now/) | Check whether this intake accepts someone relocating from abroad, the school location and current requirements. Distinguish ongoing recruitment from a vacancy at a named centre. |
| In person, colleges and universities | [China University Jobs](https://www.chinauniversityjobs.com/jobs/) and [jobs.ac.uk](https://www.jobs.ac.uk/) | Follow leads through to the institution's English HR site. Search for EAP (English for Academic Purposes), English language lecturer or ESL instructor. Find the official form or vacancy-specific email and closing date. |

### Targeted searches

- Online: combine “online business English trainer”, “adult ESL tutor” or “corporate English instructor” with “China”, “remote from abroad” and a known provider. Also search 成人商务英语 线上外教 招聘 and 企业英语培训 远程 讲师. Check where the teacher must sit.
- In person: search “English language lecturer”, “EAL teacher” or “English for Academic Purposes”, a city or “China”, and “vacancies” or “apply”. Also search 高校 外籍英语教师 招聘 and 国际学校 英语教师 招聘. Open named universities' and schools' own careers pages.
- Overseas fallback: search the exact employer + title + job reference on international boards. Open the result and verify that it points to the same active opening. When available, use a recruiting contact expressly designated by the school for that posting.
- To check the scope of cross-border school-age tutoring, start with the [public policy Q&A](https://www.shanghai.gov.cn/nw12344/20210827/a582b5c78d0c47058a1cf0dc7cbf033c.html) and look for current national or local education authority guidance. For a question about work permission, consult [official work guidance](https://english.www.gov.cn/2025special/bizexpatsinchina2025/) and local guidance. Give personal credential or institution referrals only when requested.
